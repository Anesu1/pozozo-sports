POZOZO SPORTS · IDENTITY

The seam mark

One shape doing three jobs: the O of Pozozo, the seam of a ball, and the Z the name turns on. A single closed path — no gradients, no effects, no second colour.

How it is built

A 46-unit disc on a 100-unit grid. The seam is knocked out, not drawn on top, so the mark stays one object in any colour and reverses without edits. Bars are 12 units — thick enough to survive a 16px favicon.

01
A ball, not a circle
The disc is the O and the ball at once. Nothing else in the mark exists to decorate it.
02
The seam is the Z
Read it as a panel seam or as the letter. Both are true, and neither has to be explained.
03
Cut, never added
Knocked out with an even-odd fill, so the background shows through and the mark never needs a second colour.

Clear space

Keep a margin of one seam-bar — a twelfth of the mark's width — clear on every side. Nothing sits inside it.

Never

Rotate it. The seam is horizontal because the ball is at rest.
Squash it. A ball that isn't round isn't the logo.
Sit it on a tone it can't beat. The seam has to read, always.
